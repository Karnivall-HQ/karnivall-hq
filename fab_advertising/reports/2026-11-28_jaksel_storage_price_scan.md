## Reads
1. **Fab sits at the market price for small units, not below it** (INFERRED, 2 data points). Fab small 197k/m³ vs Tiger Half 200k/m³: same price per volume. Fab is cheaper per floor m² than Digudang (395k vs 500k *(calc)*), though Digudang is a different area and its height is unknown.
2. **"Murah" is weak as a lone claim; "no deposit / no minimum / free pickup" is the edge.** Tiger also says "no long-term contracts", so "fleksibel" alone doesn't set Fab apart. None of the sources found publish free pickup or a no-deposit line next to the price (from these pages only; not verified with the operators).
3. **The real low-price anchor is SpaceHub at 388–400k** (Thamrin, size unknown, probably a locker). An ad saying "termurah" could be beaten on headline price → keep copy on "Rp750rb all-in, tanpa deposit" rather than "paling murah".
4. **Fab's 750 vs 800 conflict matters more than the price level:** 50k = 6.7% *(calc)*, the same gap as Fab vs Tiger Half (700k). One consistent number is worth more than undercutting. That supports site ask W2 (OUTBOX #2), and holds whether or not it is applied.

**Read (my interpretation; small samples, so these are hypotheses, not proof):**
1. **Direct beats subtle.** Ads that say *what it is + price + where* (Ad015, Ad007, 3-21) convert. The soft lifestyle hook (KENJI-001, yoga mat) gets low CTR and 0 leads.
2. **Concrete pain beats generic.** "Apartemen malah jadi gudang?" and the item list (sofa, kulkas, koper) have the highest CTRs (2.9–3.3%) and the cheapest leads, but only Rp 60–120k spend each.
3. **Price anchor matters.** Every lead-producing ad shows Rp750rb or Rp800rb.

## Batch 01: 6 new ads (copy ready, Bahasa Indonesia)
_`\n` = line break in the primary text._
Rules for every ad: name the product ("gudang self-storage") in the first line, show the price anchor, show the location, one CTA. Visuals: real unit photos (clean, bright, padlock) beat illustrations. Uses only facts already in live ads: Jakarta Selatan (Kuningan & Sudirman), from Rp750rb/bulan with code FAB750 on WhatsApp, landing `fabwarehouses.com`.

### A1 · "Apartemen jadi gudang" (scale the 204F winner angle into a single image)
- **Hook / first line:** 😣 Kamar apartemen malah jadi gudang?
- **Body:** Sewa apartemen di Jakarta itu mahal. Sayang kalau satu kamar cuma buat numpuk barang.\n📦 Pindahkan barang yang jarang dipakai ke gudang self-storage Fab. Unit pribadi, bersih, terkunci.\n📍 Jakarta Selatan (Kuningan & Sudirman)\n💰 Harga promo launching Rp750rb/bulan (kode FAB750 via WhatsApp).
- **Headline:** Kamar Kembali Lega, Barang Tetap Aman
- **Visual:** split image: cluttered apartment room vs. the same room empty; small inset of a Fab unit with padlock.
- **Hypothesis:** the 204F angle (CPL Rp 29k, CTR 2.94%) holds at higher spend.

### A2 · "Muat apa saja?" (item list, from 3-21)
- **Hook:** Cari gudang self-storage di Jakarta? Ini yang muat di unit Fab 👇
- **Body:** Barang kecil: box dokumen, baju, sepatu, koper.\nBarang besar: kulkas, lemari, rak, sepeda (sofa & kasur: unit Medium ke atas).\n🔒 Unit pribadi, bersih & terang, pintunya terkunci.\n📍 Kuningan & Sudirman, Jakarta Selatan\n💰 Harga promo launching Rp750rb/bulan (kode FAB750 via WhatsApp).
- **Headline:** Sewa Gudang Self-storage Jakarta, Mulai Rp750rb
- **Visual:** carousel, one card per item type inside a real unit (sofa, kulkas, boxes, sepeda), final card = price + location.
- **Hypothesis:** concrete items answer "will my stuff fit?", the main pre-lead question. 3-21 had the best CTR (3.30%).

### A3 · "Lagi renovasi / pindahan" (new life-event angle)
- **Hook:** 🔨 Rumah lagi renovasi? Barang mau ditaruh di mana?
- **Body:** Simpan dulu perabot dan barang di gudang self-storage Fab selama renovasi atau pindahan. Sewa bulanan, tidak perlu kontrak tahunan.*\n📍 Kuningan & Sudirman, Jakarta Selatan\n💰 Mulai Rp750rb/bulan (kode FAB750 via WhatsApp).
- **Headline:** Gudang Sementara Saat Renovasi & Pindahan
- **Visual:** furniture wrapped in plastic inside a clean unit.
- **Hypothesis:** time-bound need → high intent. *⚠️ Confirm the minimum rental period before using "tidak perlu kontrak tahunan"; drop that line if it isn't true.

### A4 · "Bersih, terang, terkunci" v2 (iterate the Ad015 winner, don't replace it)
- **Hook:** 🔒 Gudang self-storage yang bersih, terang, dan pintunya terkunci, di tengah Jakarta.
- **Body:** Same body as Ad015. Swap the image only: a real walk-up shot of the unit door + padlock, with "Mulai Rp750rb/bln" overlaid.
- **Headline:** Sewa Unit Self-storage Bersih & Terang di Jakarta Selatan
- **Hypothesis:** keeps the proven message and resets frequency/CTR decay with a new visual.

### A5 · "Kantor / usaha" (B2B angle: stock, documents)
- **Hook:** 🗂️ Kantor penuh box dokumen dan stok barang?
- **Body:** Pindahkan arsip, stok, dan peralatan ke gudang self-storage Fab. Kantor lebih lega tanpa sewa ruang tambahan.\n📍 Kuningan & Sudirman, dekat area perkantoran Jakarta Selatan\n💰 Mulai Rp750rb/bulan (kode FAB750 via WhatsApp).
- **Headline:** Gudang Arsip & Stok untuk Kantor di Jakarta Selatan
- **Visual:** shelves of labelled document boxes inside a unit.
- **Hypothesis:** Kuningan/Sudirman = office district. Untested segment, possibly higher lead value.

### A6 · "Barang di rumah keluarga" (scale KENJI-020, sharpen the CTA)
- **Hook:** 🤝 Barangmu masih numpang di rumah orang tua?
- **Body:** Sudah waktunya punya tempat sendiri untuk barang-barangmu. Gudang self-storage Fab: unit pribadi, terkunci, loading barang bisa Sabtu & Minggu juga.\n📍 Kuningan & Sudirman, Jakarta Selatan\n💰 Harga promo launching Rp750rb/bulan (kode FAB750 via WhatsApp).
- **Headline:** Gudang Pribadi untuk Barangmu, Mulai Rp750rb
- **Hypothesis:** KENJI-020 has good CTR (2.09%) but its copy has no price; adding the price anchor should lift CVR. *11/12 14:02: "bisa diakses saat perlu" replaced with the page's own "Sabtu & Minggu bisa" loading claim (page has 0× "akses").*

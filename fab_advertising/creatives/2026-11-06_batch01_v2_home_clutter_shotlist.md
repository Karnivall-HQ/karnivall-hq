## How to shoot (10 min, any phone)
1. Vertical, 9:16, no filter, normal room light. Don't tidy first: the mess is the point.
2. Open each photo in the Instagram story editor (don't post), add the stickers below with the default "Classic"/"Typewriter" text style + one yellow highlight, then save to camera roll. That reproduces the Image087 look exactly.
3. Send the 6 saved images to Fuku's folder (`fab_advertising/creatives/2026-11-06_shoot/`) or straight to Markus for upload.

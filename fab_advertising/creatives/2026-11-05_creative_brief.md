## 🔍 The pattern
1. **Raw phone photos beat polished design, 3–10× on CPL.** Every ad under Rp 70k is an unedited vertical phone shot (750×1334) with native Instagram-story text stickers. Every ad over Rp 200k is a polished, rendered or AI-looking graphic (941×1672 PNG). People trust what looks real: "this is the actual warehouse."
2. **Show the real facility.** The yellow epoxy floor, white units with navy frames, unit badges (D7, C1) and padlocks appear in every winner. That's proof it exists, it's clean and it's secure.
3. **The price is always on the image** ("hanya dari 800rb/bulan 👇", "750rb /bulan"), with a 👇 pointing to the CTA.
4. **Concrete big items** ("Lemari, Kulkas, Sofa, Meja, Box, Kardus") beat abstract benefits ("Ukuran bisa disesuaikan", "Matras siap. Ruangnya belum.").
5. **Apartment pain** ("Apartemen yang penuhin kamar", "barang menumpuk di Apartemen") shows up in the 3 best performers. Jakarta apartment dwellers are the core buyer.
6. **The yellow tag/sticker** on the price is the most-repeated visual element in winners.

**Rule for the next batch:** shoot it on a phone, at the warehouse, with story-style stickers. No renders, no stock, no AI lifestyle scenes.

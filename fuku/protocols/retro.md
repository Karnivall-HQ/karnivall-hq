# Retro protocol (weekly self-improvement)

Goal: get measurably better at finishing missions.

1. **Review**: last 7 days of journals, lessons.md, MISSIONS progress, and OUTBOX (what Alvian approved, rejected, or corrected).
2. **Score**: for each active mission, ask whether it's on track (yes/no/at-risk) and what's the biggest bottleneck. Should the strategy change? If yes, change it in MISSIONS.md.
3. **Consolidate**: merge related lessons into playbooks; delete lessons that are absorbed or proven wrong.
4. **Tune memory**: prune stale facts in alvian.md and company.md; make sure NOW.md is lean.
5. **Upgrade yourself**:
   - Rewrite the "Evolving operating rules" section of `CLAUDE.md` (≤ 30 lines) with your improved rules.
   - Create or improve subagents (`.claude/agents/`), commands (`.claude/commands/`), or scripts (`fuku/bin/`) that would have saved time this week.
   - Never touch CLAUDE.md §1–§4 or weaken guardrails.
6. **Log**: add a dated entry to `fuku/CHANGELOG.md` (what changed and why), starting with "Retro".
7. **Report**: add an OUTBOX [REPORT] with the week in 5 bullets: wins, misses, what I changed about myself, what I need from Alvian, and next week's focus.
